Norie is a personal tasks, reminders and finance app at norie-bliq.vercel.app.

What we collect

If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and name (the basic profile Google shares). We store the data you create in Norie: tasks, reminders, settings, and — only if you use the finance feature — the bank text messages you choose to forward (CBE, telebirr, Awash) and the amounts parsed from them.

Google user data

Norie uses Google Sign-In only to identify you. We request the basic scopes openid, email and profile and nothing else. We use your email address only as your account identifier. We do not read your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, contacts or any other Google data, we do not transfer Google user data to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising. Norie's use of information received from Google follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Bank messages

If you set up automatic capture, an iPhone Shortcut on your own phone forwards only messages from CBE, 127 (telebirr) and Awash Bank to your private Norie account. Messages from any other sender are ignored. Message text is used to compute amounts and balances and is deleted from the cloud inbox once your app imports it (and in any case after 30 days).

How it is used

Only to run the app for you: sync between your devices, send Telegram reminders you set up, and show your finance totals. We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with third parties.

Where it is stored

On your device, and, if you sign in, in a private database hosted by Supabase. Each account can only read its own data. Telegram reminders are sent through the bot token you provide.

Your choices

You can use Norie without signing in (data stays on your phone). Signed-in users can delete all their cloud data in Settings → Account → More → Delete my cloud data, or log out at any time.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept until you delete it. Use Settings → Account → More → Delete my cloud data to erase everything stored in the cloud, or email us to request deletion of your account.
